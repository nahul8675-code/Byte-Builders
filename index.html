About Byte-Builders

Byte-Builders is an image-first educational project created to make hardware electronics, coding, and cybersecurity clear and practical for students and beginners. Every tutorial includes a visual diagram, a complete list of items used, verified wiring tables, and full copy-paste code.

🛡️ Ethical Cybersecurity Rule: All Linux and cybersecurity tutorials are strictly for educational and defensive purposes. Only test commands on your own computer, VirtualBox labs, or authorized practice platforms.
